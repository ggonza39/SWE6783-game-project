# Pixel Rebellion: game audio

Adds sound to the actual two-level game. This update starts from main
`66f9dfac78a68e6372c1de74ea91dfa9458b1c69`, including the merged WebGL flicker and
replay performance fixes. It does not replace scenes or alter difficulty,
damage, mines, stamina, saves/checkpoints, telemetry, or the two-level ending.

## Sound design

- Facility Breach: restrained minor-key electronic stealth theme.
- Crimson Depths: a darker industrial pulse with a separate original score.
- Recorded pistol shots, with quiet crouch/sprint footsteps and body impacts.
- Mines stay completely silent while concealed. Triggering one starts accelerating
  warning beeps; detonation stops that warning and plays a layered boom/debris sound.
- Laser phase changes charge and energize nearby hardware; actual laser contact
  produces an electrical burn. Guard deaths use a body impact; fatal mines add
  fragment impacts. Gunfire does not use an explosion sound.
- Downloads, keycards, successful hacks, door releases, sector checkpoints,
  hiding, alerts, sprint exhaustion, deployment, level clear and final victory
  have their own cues. Restoring secured objectives does not replay pickup sounds.

## Mixing and controls

Settings contains **Sound On/Muted**, **Music** and **Sound Effects** controls.
Preferences persist across game launches and New Game. Older profiles receive
sensible audio defaults without losing progress or existing options.

The default music/effects settings are 55% / 75%. Music stays under effects and
briefly ducks for strong impacts and important success cues. Up to ten effect
voices share a 0.62 gain budget; music has a separate maximum 0.30 budget.
Imported peaks remain below -1 dBFS. Distances/panning soften nearby-world sounds,
and rapid gunfire/UI events are rate limited. All samples are pre-rendered and
imported as PCM, with no runtime DSP or additional recording overhead.

Pause stops world effects and interaction/alarm loops; menu feedback still works.
Replay creates a new scene-local sound pool, so old shots and alarms do not leak
into the next attempt. The existing security archive is a **visual** death replay;
it does not record/replay an audio track. The original live hit/death is audible.

WebGL audio starts after a click or keypress, as required by browser autoplay.
If the Unity Editor is silent, check the Game view's Mute Audio toggle and the
computer's output device/volume. A website must serve a newly rebuilt WebGL
player to include the audio: these source changes cannot update an old hosted build.

## Verification and local acceptance

Offline checks compile the real runtime with inert Unity/audio API stand-ins.
The actual analytics uploader is excluded, and no fake survey/player sessions
are submitted. Existing campaign, menu, replay, impact and save checks are run
alongside new audio event/mixing checks. `tools/verify_game_audio.py` checks the
actual 32 WAV files for hashes, non-silence, inventory, loop seams and peak budget.
These checks do not replace hearing the real Unity or packaged/browser build.

Before committing:

1. Open Facility Breach; click the menu. Check its background theme and Settings
   volume/mute controls. Enter a mission; test footsteps, download, canceling E,
   hack, pause/Help, TAB map, a guard shot/hit/death and replay.
2. Extract from Level 1. Confirm its level-clear cue and direct transition into
   Crimson Depths, with the different theme and the normal saved checkpoint.
3. In Level 2, walk into one hidden mine. Confirm silence before trigger, warning
   after trigger, then a boom at detonation. Escape one and be hit by one. At low
   health, check the fatal mine visuals with the corresponding blast/fragment sound.
4. Test laser phase audio and a laser death; compare with guard death. There should
   be no explosion sound from a bullet. Test a sector console/checkpoint and Replay.
5. Complete Level 2: final victory should sound once. Repeat key events in Hard;
   sound levels should match Easy. The existing 10s/20s stamina budgets are unchanged.
6. Relaunch and check saved volume, difficulty and checkpoint. Make a fresh Windows
   build and WebGL build; click the browser game and confirm both music and effects.
