# Final gameplay recording order — capture after audio acceptance

The assignment requires **first mid-demo versus final game side by side**, with
spoken comparison. Keep that as the main edit. Label the sides only BEFORE and
AFTER, retain full gameplay frames, and use little text beneath them. Do not put
editing directions or narration instructions in the video.

Record clean 1920x1080 gameplay with computer audio on and the cursor outside
important HUD elements. Keep normal speed. Leave 5–8 seconds around menu actions
and 10–20 seconds around a feature so narration has room to breathe. Do not
stretch small footage or crop away the health, sprint, objective or buttons.

| Order | Real footage to capture | Spoken purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Main menu, both level cards, Easy/Hard choice, Continue highlight | Introduce two levels and clearly show where New Game begins and Continue resumes. |
| 2 | Settings, sound controls, actual Help/controls, ESC pause, TAB map; cancel a restart | Explain the controls and navigation before the action. |
| 3 | Level 1 Easy: keycard, data collection, released E, hack and extraction | Compare the original interaction flow with the completed version. |
| 4 | Level 1 Hard: patrol pressure, guard shooting/death, replay and completed run | Demonstrate difficulty through real behavior. |
| 5 | Level 1 extraction directly entering Level 2 | Establish the campaign transition without a misleading jump cut. |
| 6 | Level 2 Easy: sector layout, data, local console and saved checkpoint | Explain the larger level's new structure. |
| 7 | A mine triggered and escaped; a mine hit; fatal blast with actual scattering | Give each cause and its visual/audio response time to be seen. |
| 8 | Laser warning/active cycle and actual electrical death; replay | Demonstrate a different hazard and a different death cause. |
| 9 | Level 2 Hard: pressure, conserving sprint, cover, guard death and retry | Compare Easy and Hard while preserving the full HUD. |
| 10 | Checkpoint recovery, final collections/hack/exit and real campaign-complete screen | Prove recovery and show the final success. |

During a dedicated final-game difficulty comparison, Level 1 may be on the left
and Level 2 on the right, clearly labeled with each current mode; retain a small
BEFORE thumbnail. This is an additional section, not a replacement for the
required BEFORE/AFTER comparison. Avoid a four-gameplay-window grid: the HUD
would become too small to read.

Capture one uninterrupted successful campaign too, in case the course's “Full
Gameplay” requirement needs the complete run or a longer appendix. Keep the
original mid-demo footage as the genuine baseline. Do not invent missing death,
help or ending demonstrations, or add unsynchronized sound to old footage and
present it as the updated game. Record narration separately against the final
edit and supply its timecoded guide as a separate document.
